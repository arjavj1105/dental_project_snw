Oral Care Tips

Expert tips from our dental team to keep your smile healthy and bright

🪥

Proper Brushing Technique

  • ✓ Hold brush at 45-degree angle to gums
  • ✓ Use gentle, short strokes
  • ✓ Brush for full 2 minutes
  • ✓ Don't forget the tongue!
🪮

Soft Bristles Matter

  • ✓ Always choose soft bristles
  • ✓ Replace brush every 3 months
  • ✓ Don't press too hard
  • ✓ Electric brushes are great too
👅

Tongue Cleaning

  • ✓ Use a tongue scraper daily
  • ✓ Clean from back to front
  • ✓ Reduces bad breath by 70%
  • ✓ Part of complete oral care
🧵

Flossing Daily

  • ✓ Floss at least once daily
  • ✓ Use 18 inches of floss
  • ✓ Curve around each tooth
  • ✓ Don't skip the back teeth
💧

Mouthwash Care

  • ✓ Use after brushing & flossing
  • ✓ Swish for 30-60 seconds
  • ✓ Don't rinse with water after
  • ✓ Choose alcohol-free options
🍎

Diet & Habits

  • ✓ Limit sugary drinks & snacks
  • ✓ Drink plenty of water
  • ✓ Wait 30 mins to brush after eating
  • ✓ Avoid tobacco products
💡

Pro Tip from Our Dentists

Never brush immediately after eating acidic foods! Wait at least 30 minutes. Acids soften your enamel temporarily, and brushing right away can cause damage. Rinse with water instead and brush later.
